# Dev notes — 9 September 2026, 20:09 BST

## Contract timing: let the first minute breathe

The contracts work as a solo progression loop, but the opening needs fewer competing instructions. Establish running, buying trainers and collecting the first crate before introducing another job system.

Currently, contracts unlock after ten taps or any technology purchase. That can happen in seconds. The next-move card separately recommends a contract after opening a shop with no runners or completed contracts, or as the fallback when other purchase goals run out. These two entry points need the same timing rule.

**Recommendation: reveal contracts after 90 seconds of active gameplay.** Retain the existing ten-taps-or-technology requirement. Use the saved active-play clock, so leaving the browser closed does not skip the introduction. This also leaves some space before the protection explanations begin at two minutes.

- Hide both the contract section and contract recommendations until eligible. Continue showing the next useful purchase instead; if there is none, omit the next-move card temporarily.
- Reveal quietly, without a modal or an automatic scroll. If a tutorial is open, wait until it closes. The first crate already provides the big early moment.
- Introduce it as “An airlock needs feeding” with “Complete deliveries for an extra payout. No recruits needed.” Let View contract retain its scroll and shimmer.
- Preserve access for saves with an accepted contract or previous completions, including recovered games. Reloads and franchising must not restart the introduction.
- Once accepted, make the next-move card reflect the actual job: bunker name, deliveries remaining, then “Payment ready.” The generic “Keep the airlocks fed” becomes repetitive when it never acknowledges progress.

Check the 89/90-second boundary, hidden-tab time, first-crate overlap, returning saves, and that View contract can never point at a hidden section.

## The animated operation: give it an understandable home

The panel currently illustrates existing progress. It does not start a job or grant a bonus. The shop name is yours; “Ministry Lunch Portal on the route” names your highest owned delivery technology; “The Before-Times” is your current zone; “AUTOMATION AUTHORISED” is flavour text indicating technology ownership. The animation loops independently of actual delivery speed.

That explains the confusion: it looks like a new interactive system, but its purpose is to celebrate the business you already built. The four labels need a little more context.

**Recommendation: move it to the top of Portfolio as “Your operation.”** It would give an otherwise sparse early Portfolio something personal and alive. Keep it there as the portfolio grows, with runners and franchises beneath it, so its location remains predictable.

- Before opening a shop: “Your delivery route,” the citizen's name, and “Just you and a delivery bag.” After opening one, promote the shop name to the title.
- Label the technology “Best delivery tech: Ministry Lunch Portal” and the location “Zone: The Before-Times.” Let the existing dystopian status line remain secondary flavour.
- Add a plain explanation: “A live overview of your delivery business. Buy technology in Operations to grow it.”
- Show actual automatic deliveries per second beside the scene, using the existing production calculation. This gives it useful meaning without implying that each animated crossing equals a paid delivery.
- Use one helpful action, “Upgrade technology,” returning to the technology section in Operations. Preserve reduced-motion support and allow long shop names to wrap cleanly.
- In an empty Portfolio, follow it with one short invitation to grow the business instead of several separate empty sections. Once people join, show the real runner and franchise content.

Moving the scene also frees space near Run. Review the remaining social card at full width so it does not become a large recruitment advert. The main screen should make the next delivery or purchase easy to find; Portfolio should show what those actions have built.

## Suggested order

1. Add the shared contract timing rule and progress-aware next-move copy.
2. Move and relabel the operation scene in Portfolio, with its production figure and upgrade action.
3. Review the first three active minutes with a fresh citizen and revisit Portfolio with a mature save on desktop and mobile.

These are recommendations for the next iteration; this note does not change gameplay.
